Magic World Poster

A desktop publishing tool for creators and social media managers. It lets an operator prepare a video once and publish it to the social accounts they manage — Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts, and TikTok accounts — on a schedule they choose.

What the tool does

  • Keeps a local queue of videos with a caption, a content category and a publishing time.
  • Publishes each video to the connected platforms through the official platform APIs (Meta Graph API and the TikTok Content Posting API).
  • Shows a per-platform status for every item, so the operator can see what has been published and what is still waiting.
  • Labels AI-generated content where the destination platform supports a disclosure flag, and adds the disclosure to captions.

How publishing works

  1. The operator signs in with the account they want to publish to. The app receives an access token through the platform's official authorization flow and never sees a password.
  2. The operator picks a video from the local queue and chooses a destination: a Facebook Page, an Instagram professional account, or a TikTok account.
  3. Before anything is sent to TikTok, the operator reviews a preview of the post and must explicitly select a privacy status and confirm their consent to publish.
  4. The video is uploaded to the platform and published. The app reports the final status returned by the platform.

TikTok integration

The application uses TikTok's Content Posting API. It requests the user.info.basic, video.publish and video.upload scopes, which are used only to read the authorised account's basic profile information, to publish a video the operator has explicitly approved, and to send a video to the account's TikTok inbox as a draft.

Content is published only after the operator selects the privacy status and presses the publish button. The application does not post anything on its own without that confirmation, and it respects the account's available privacy levels, comment, duet and stitch settings.
