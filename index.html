One queue, every channel

Built for creators and social media managers who publish the same short video in several places and want a clear record of what went out.

1

Local queue

Keep videos, captions, categories and publish times together on your own machine. Nothing is uploaded until a post is due or you press publish.

2

Per-platform control

Choose Facebook, Instagram, TikTok — or any combination — for each item. See a separate status for every destination.

3

Official APIs only

Publishing uses the Meta Graph API and the TikTok Content Posting API. You sign in through each platform; the app never sees your password.

4

Human approval

Before anything is sent to TikTok you review a preview and choose the privacy level. The app does not post in the background without that confirmation.

5

Safe retries

Failed uploads retry with backoff. Idempotency checks stop the same caption from being published twice after a restart.

6

AI disclosure

Where the destination supports a disclosure flag, AI-generated content is labelled — and the disclosure is added to the caption too.

How publishing works

Four steps from a folder of videos to posts that are live on the platforms you choose.

  1. Connect your accounts Sign in with the Facebook Page, Instagram professional account and TikTok account you are allowed to publish to. Access is granted through each platform's official authorization flow.
  2. Add videos to the queue Give each video a caption, a category and a time. You can pick one destination or all of them, and reorder or reschedule at any point.
  3. Review before publishing Open an item to check the caption, thumbnail and destination. For TikTok, choose the privacy status and confirm that you are ready to publish.
  4. Track the result Each platform reports back its own status and post identifier. Anything that fails is queued for a retry and shown clearly in the app.

Supported destinations

The same workflow across the three platforms where short-form video matters most.

Facebook Page Publish to a Page you manage through the Meta Graph API, with scheduling and status reporting.
Instagram Reels Publish Reels to an Instagram professional account, including caption and cover handling.
TikTok Publish directly where the account allows it, or send the video to the TikTok inbox as a draft to finish in the app.
TikTok draft flow. TikTok's API can place a video in your account's TikTok inbox as a draft until your app has direct-publish access. Magic World Poster handles that automatically and marks the item so you know to open TikTok and finish the post.

Frequently asked

A few things people ask before connecting an account.

Where is my data stored?

The queue, tokens and publishing history are stored locally on the machine where you run the app. Video files are only transmitted to the platform you choose when a post is published. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Does the app publish without my approval?

No. Scheduled items are uploaded at their time, but TikTok publishing requires you to review the post, select a privacy status and confirm consent. Facebook and Instagram posts follow the queue you created.

Do I need a developer account?

You need to authorize the app with each platform once. The connection uses the platform's standard OAuth flow; you never type your password into Magic World Poster.

What happens if a platform is down?

The item is marked as pending or failed, and the app retries with backoff. Completed platforms are not posted again thanks to idempotency checks.
