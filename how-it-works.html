How it works

A complete walkthrough — from authorizing an account to checking the published post.

Magic World Poster runs entirely on the operator's machine. It prepares a post, uploads it through the platform's official API, and records the result. The steps below describe what happens at each stage.

  1. 1 · Connect the accounts you manage The operator chooses Facebook, Instagram or TikTok and completes that platform's authorization screen. The platform returns an access token, which the app stores locally. Passwords are never requested or stored. Only accounts the operator is permitted to manage are accepted by the platform.
  2. 2 · Build the local queue Videos are added from disk. For each item the operator sets a caption, a content category, the destinations and a publishing time. The queue can be sorted and edited at any point before publishing.
  3. 3 · Review and confirm Opening an item shows a preview of the video, its caption and the destination accounts. For TikTok, the publish wizard also asks for a privacy status and an explicit confirmation that the operator consents to publish the video.
  4. 4 · Publish through the official API Facebook and Instagram uploads use the Meta Graph API. TikTok uploads use the Content Posting API. The app sends one upload at a time, waits for the platform to report success, and stores the returned post identifier.
  5. 5 · Verify the result The queue shows a status per destination. Successful posts are marked as posted. Failures show the platform's error and are retried with backoff. If an item is partially published, only the failed destinations are retried.

The TikTok workflow

TikTok's Content Posting API supports two paths. Magic World Poster handles both.

Direct publish

When the authorized account and the app's approved access allow direct publishing, the video is posted to the account. The operator still chooses the privacy level and confirms before the request is sent.

Inbox draft

Otherwise the video is uploaded to the account's TikTok inbox as a draft. The app shows a draft marker in the queue and copies the caption to the clipboard. The operator opens TikTok, reviews the draft, pastes the caption and publishes it there.

Why drafts? TikTok limits the number of unposted drafts in an inbox. When the limit is reached, the app pauses TikTok uploads for that item and retries later, while Facebook and Instagram continue normally.

Scheduling and recovery

What the engine does while you are not looking.

Due checks

The engine checks the queue on a short interval. When an item's time arrives, it is handed to a single upload worker so requests do not overlap.

Catch-up

If the app was closed at the scheduled time, overdue items are published when it starts again, in queue order.

Backoff

A failed upload waits before retrying, with a cap on attempts. The reason is written to the log and shown on the item.
