Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Magic World Poster (â€œthe applicationâ€) is a desktop publishing tool used by an operator to publish video content to social accounts the operator is authorized to manage. This policy explains what information the application handles, why it handles it, and how it can be removed.

1. Information the application handles

DataWhy it is needed
Account identifiers and basic profile information (display name, account ID) from Meta and TikTok To confirm which account a video will be published to and to show the operator who is connected.
Access and refresh tokens issued by Meta and TikTok To call the official publishing APIs on the operator's behalf.
Video files, captions, content categories and scheduled publishing times chosen by the operator To prepare and publish the requested posts.
Platform responses such as post identifiers and publishing status To display results and to avoid publishing the same video twice.

The application does not request or store platform passwords, and it does not access private messages, followers, or any content other than what the operator publishes.

2. TikTok data

When the operator connects a TikTok account, the application uses TikTok's official authorization flow and requests the user.info.basic, video.publish and video.upload scopes. These are used solely to:

  • read the authorized account's basic profile (display name and avatar) so the operator can confirm the correct account before publishing;
  • publish a video that the operator has previewed, for which the operator has explicitly chosen a privacy status and confirmed consent to publish; and
  • send a video to that account's TikTok inbox as a draft, when the operator chooses the draft option or when direct publishing is not available.

The application does not publish to TikTok without that explicit confirmation. Information obtained from TikTok is used only to provide these publishing features and is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for advertising or any other purpose.

3. Meta data

When the operator connects a Facebook Page or an Instagram professional account, the application uses Meta's official APIs to read the pages the operator manages and to publish the videos the operator requests. It does not store Facebook or Instagram passwords.

4. Where the data is stored

The queue, tokens and publishing status are stored locally on the machine where the operator runs the application, apart from video uploads that are necessarily transmitted to the destination platform when a post is published. Access tokens are not committed to any public source code and are not printed into ordinary application logs.

5. Sharing

Information is sent only to the platform(s) the operator selects for a given post (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok). The application contains no advertising SDK and no third-party analytics. It does not sell personal information.

6. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as needed to run the scheduled posts and to keep the publishing history accurate. The operator can remove any queued item, which deletes its local records and video copy. To request deletion of all information held by the application, including stored account identifiers, tokens, and publishing metadata, use the contact details below. Requests are actioned promptly, subject to any legal obligations. Information that has already been transmitted to a platform is also governed by that platform's own policies.

7. Security

Reasonable technical measures are taken to protect the information the application handles, including keeping tokens out of public source code and out of ordinary logs, and limiting access to the machine where the application runs.
