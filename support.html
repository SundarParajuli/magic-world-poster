Support

Help with setup, publishing, account access and data requests.

If you run into a problem, the fastest route is the user guide and the FAQ. If the answer is not there, use the contact options below.

Contact

Email: sparajuli@miu.edu
GitHub: github.com/SundarParajuli

Please include:

  • the platform involved (Facebook, Instagram or TikTok);
  • the queue item's title or identifier, if there is one;
  • the status and error text shown in the app or the local log;
  • the approximate time the problem occurred.

Do not send access tokens or passwords. Nobody associated with the app will ask for them.

Response times

Support is handled on a best-effort basis, normally within two business days. Security reports and data-deletion requests are prioritized.

Data deletion

Magic World Poster stores its queue, tokens and publishing history locally on the computer where it runs. To delete data:

  1. Individual items: remove the item in the app. Its local record and video copy are deleted.
  2. Everything: email the address above with the subject “Data deletion”. Describe the machine or installation the request relates to. All locally stored account identifiers, tokens, queue records and logs associated with that installation will be removed.
  3. Platform-side data: content already published to Facebook, Instagram or TikTok is controlled by that platform. Delete it through the platform's own tools, and revoke the app's access from the platform's security or connected-apps settings.

See the Privacy Policy for the full description of what is stored and why.

Report a security issue

If you believe you have found a security problem, email the address above with “Security” in the subject line. Include enough detail to reproduce the issue, and please do not publish it publicly before it has been reviewed.

Account access

To disconnect an account, use the app's account settings or revoke access from the platform:

  • Meta: Settings → Apps and websites → remove the app.
  • TikTok: Settings → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions.
