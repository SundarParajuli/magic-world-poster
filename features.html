Features

Everything the app does, from the first import to the final platform status.

Magic World Poster is deliberately focused. It is not a full social-media management suite; it is a reliable local queue and publishing bridge for short-form video, with a clear audit trail for every post.

Q

Local video queue

Add a video once and keep its file, caption, category, target platforms and scheduled time together. Reorder or reschedule items at any time before they publish.

S

Flexible scheduling

Pick an exact date and time for each item. The engine checks every few seconds and publishes the next due post, catching up on anything that became overdue while the app was closed.

P

Per-platform status

Every item records a separate result for Facebook, Instagram and TikTok, including post identifiers and error messages, so you always know what actually went live.

D

TikTok inbox drafts

When direct publishing is not available for your app, the video is uploaded to your TikTok inbox as a draft. The app marks the row and copies the caption so you can finish it in the TikTok app.

R

Retries with backoff

Temporary network or API failures are retried automatically. If one platform succeeds and another fails, only the failed destination is attempted again.

I

Idempotency guards

Caption fingerprints and stored post identifiers prevent the same item from being published twice after a restart or an interrupted upload.

A

AI-generated disclosure

Mark an item as AI-generated and the app uses the destination platform's disclosure option where available, and adds a disclosure line to the caption as a fallback.

L

Local-first privacy

Queue data, tokens and logs stay on your machine. There is no advertising SDK, no third-party analytics and no cloud account to create.

C

Caption tools

Keep reusable niches and hashtag sets, copy captions to the clipboard, and preview exactly what will accompany each video before it publishes.

Built around explicit consent

Publishing is an action you take, not something that happens behind your back.

Review before publishing

The publish wizard shows the file, caption, destination and privacy choices. Nothing is sent to TikTok until you select a privacy status and confirm consent.

Respect for platform settings

The app requests only the scopes it needs and honours the privacy levels, comment, duet and stitch options that the platform reports for your account.

Transparent logs

Each attempt is written to a local log with the platform response, so a failure can be diagnosed without guessing. Tokens are never written to ordinary logs.

You stay in control

Disconnect an account at any time, revoke access from the platform's own settings, or remove a queue item to delete its local record and video copy.
